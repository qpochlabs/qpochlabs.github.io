This is the new home for my blog posts at [qpoch.com](https://qpoch.com), covering my work and notes on AI research — perception, planning, sim2real, novel view synthesis, and the occasional thought experiment in fundamental AI.

## How this blog works

Posts are written in plain Markdown and live in the `_posts` folder of the repository. Each file is named `YYYY-MM-DD-title.md` and starts with a small front matter block containing the post title. Everything else — the list on the home page, the post pages, dates and the RSS feed — is generated automatically.

## What to expect

Write-ups of ongoing projects, paper notes, and short explainers. For my publications and research profile, head over to [hari.qpoch.com](https://hari.qpoch.com).
